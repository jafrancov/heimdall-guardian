# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is published at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be respectful, welcome people of every background and experience level, give and
accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, Alejandro Franco, through his GitHub profile
([@jafrancov](https://github.com/jafrancov)). Reports are handled with discretion.
Vulnerabilities in the software follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
